# Evolutionary Conclusion: Helianthus annuuss (Sunflower)

## 1. Overview
*Helianthus annuuss* (sunflower) is an asterid species belonging to the Asteraceae family. Its 58 identified MIKCc-type MADS-box genes offer insights intofloral and seed development in Asteraceae.

## 2. Clade Distribution & Identification
The circular phylogenetic trees (MAFFT, MUSCLE, ClustalO alignments with NJ and aBayes) show the presence of several well-defined MIKCc subclades.

*   **AP1/FUL Clade:** Conserved clustering of *Helianthus* orthologs with AT1G69120 and AT5G60910, reflecting its core roles in floral meristem identity and achene development.
*   **AG/STK Clade:** Stably clustered with AT3G58780 (AG), reinforcing its role in reproductive organ and Asteraceae-characteristic achene identities.
*   **SEP Clade:** Significant expansion of SEP-like genes compared to *Arabidopsis*, consistent with its complex capitulum (head) floral arrangement.

## 3. Evolutionary Inference
The phylogeny place *Helianthus annuuss* as an asterid, sister to the Asterales alliance. Its MADS-box diversification reflects the family's ancient WGD events, contributing to the development of its unique composite floral structure.

## 4. Methodological Consistency
High support values (1000 replicates) in both NJ and aBayes trees across multiple alignments confirm its correct phylogenetic position and clade assignments.
