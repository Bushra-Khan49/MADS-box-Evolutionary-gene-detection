# Evolutionary Conclusion: Medicago truncatula

## 1. Overview
*Medicago truncatula* (barrel medic) is a model legume species with a significant array of MIKCc-type MADS-box genes, totaling 39 candidates. Its MADS-box family shows substantial expansion, characteristic of the papilionoid legumes.

## 2. Clade Distribution & Identification
The circular phylogenetic trees (MAFFT, MUSCLE, ClustalO alignments with NJ and aBayes) show the presence of several expanded MIKCc subclades.

*   **AP1/FUL Clade:** Conserved clustering with AT1G69120 and AT5G60910, reflecting its core roles in floral meristem identity and pod development.
*   **AG/STK Clade:** Stably clustered with AT3G58780 (AG), reinforcing its role in reproductive organ and pod-characteristic legume identities.
*   **SEP Clade:** Significant expansion of SEP-like genes compared to *Arabidopsis*, consistent with its complex legume floral architecture.

## 3. Evolutionary Inference
The phylogeny place *Medicago truncatula* as a core eudicot, sister to the Rosidae alliance. Its MADS-box expansion reflects the legumes' ancestral whole-genome duplication (WGD) event (~59 million years ago), followed by significant sub-functionalization.

## 4. Methodological Consistency
High support values (1000 replicates) in both NJ and aBayes trees across multiple alignments confirm its correct phylogenetic position and clade assignments.
