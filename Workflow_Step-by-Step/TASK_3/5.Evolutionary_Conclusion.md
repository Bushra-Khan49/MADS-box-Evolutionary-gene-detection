# Evolutionary Conclusion: Nymphaea colorata

## 1. Overview
*Nymphaea colorata* (blue lace water lily) is an early-diverging angiosperm, belonging to the order Nymphaeales. Its 22 identified MIKCc-type MADS-box genes provide key insights into early floral organ identity.

## 2. Clade Distribution & Identification
The circular phylogenetic trees (MAFFT, MUSCLE, ClustalO alignments with NJ and aBayes) show the presence of essential MADS-box clades.

*   **AP1/FUL Clade:** Foundational orthologs cluster with AT1G69120, suggesting an early establishment of the fruit development program.
*   **AG/STK Clade:** Strong clustering with AT3G58780 (AG), reflecting its core role in developing its characteristic floral anatomy.
*   **SEP Clade:** Shows a moderate expansion of SEPALLATA-like genes, which may be related to the development of its numerous perianth organs.

## 3. Evolutionary Inference
The phylogeny place *Nymphaea colorata* as an early-diverging angiosperm, sister to the Mesangiospermae. The absence of many later WGD events is reflected in its relatively small gene count (22) compared to eudicots.

## 4. Methodological Consistency
High support values (1000 replicates) in both NJ and aBayes trees across multiple alignments confirm the evolutionary stability of the results.
