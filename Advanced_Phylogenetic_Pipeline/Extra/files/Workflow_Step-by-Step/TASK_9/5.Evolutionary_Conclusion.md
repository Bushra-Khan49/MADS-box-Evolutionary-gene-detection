# Evolutionary Conclusion: Nelumbo nucifera (Sacred Lotus)

## 1. Overview
*Nelumbo nucifera* (sacred lotus) is an early-diverging eudicot, offering a critical evolutionary link between basal angiosperms and core eudicots. Our analysis identified 47 MIKCc-type MADS-box genes, reflecting its unique phylogenetic position and ancient genome history.

## 2. Clade Distribution & Identification
The circular phylogenetic trees (MAFFT, MUSCLE, ClustalO alignments with NJ and aBayes) show the existence of core MIKCc subclades.

*   **AP1/FUL Clade:** Conserved clustering of *Nelumbo* orthologs with AT1G69120 and AT5G60910, reflecting its core roles in floral meristem identity and seed development.
*   **AG/STK Clade:** Stably clustered with AT3G58780 (AG), reinforcing its role in reproductive organ and lotus-characteristic fruit identities.
*   **SEP Clade:** Significant expansion of SEP-like genes compared to *Arabidopsis*, consistent with its primitive floral architecture and aquatic lifestyle.

## 3. Evolutionary Inference
The phylogeny place *Nelumbo nucifera* as an early-diverging eudicot, sister to the Proteales. Its MADS-box diversification reflects the family's ancient whole-genome duplication (WGD) event (~65 million years ago), contributing to the development of its characteristic floral and seed traits.

## 4. Methodological Consistency
High support values (1000 replicates) in both NJ and aBayes trees across multiple alignments confirm its correct phylogenetic position and clade assignments.
