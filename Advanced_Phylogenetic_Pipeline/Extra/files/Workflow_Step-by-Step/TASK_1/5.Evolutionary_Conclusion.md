# Step 5: Evolutionary Conclusion (*Arabidopsis thaliana*)

## 1. **Overview**
The genome-wide analysis of *Arabidopsis thaliana* (TAIR10) has established a definitive reference framework of **45 MIKCc-type MADS-box genes**. These genes represent the stable evolutionary core of the MADS-box family in model eudicots, providing the anchor clades necessary for orthology assignment across all other plant species in this study.

---

## 2. **Key Evolutionary Findings**
- **Clade Conservation**: All 14 major MIKCc clades (including the well-known ABCDE model clades) are fully represented within this 45-gene set. These sequences exhibit high conservation at the MADS and K-box domains, confirming their foundational role.
- **WGD Signature**: The distribution of these **45 genes** reflects the historical Whole Genome Duplication (WGD) events (α, β, γ) in the Arabidopsis lineage, which have driven the functional diversification of floral developmental pathways.
- **Reference Standard**: As these sequences have been extensively validated in past literature (e.g., Parenicova et al., 2003), they serve as the "ground truth" for calibrating our phylogenetic trees for Basal Angiosperms, Monocots, and Magnoliids.

---

## 3. **Implications for the Research Pipeline**
The identification of this **45-gene MIKCc anchor set** provides the baseline for identifying high-confidence orthologs in less-annotated genomes. This task ensures that every downstream phylogenetic tree is anchored by known functional references.
