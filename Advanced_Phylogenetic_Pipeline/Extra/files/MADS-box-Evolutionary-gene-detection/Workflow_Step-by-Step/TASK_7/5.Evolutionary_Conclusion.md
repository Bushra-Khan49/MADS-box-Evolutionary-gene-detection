# Evolutionary Conclusion: Prunus persica (Peach)

## 1. Overview
*Prunus persica* (peach) is a core eudicot species belonging to the Rosaceae family. Its 35 identified MIKCc-type MADS-box genes offer insights into floral and fruit development in Rosaceae.

## 2. Clade Distribution & Identification
The circular phylogenetic trees (MAFFT, MUSCLE, ClustalO alignments with NJ and aBayes) show the presence of several well-defined MIKCc subclades.

*   **AP1/FUL Clade:** Conserved clustering with AT1G69120 and AT5G60910, reflecting its core roles in floral meristem identity and fleshy fruit development.
*   **AG/STK Clade:** Stably clustered with AT3G58780 (AG), reinforcing its role in reproductive organ and fleshy fruit-characteristic Rosaceae identities.
*   **SEP Clade:** Expansion of SEP-like genes, consistent with its complex floral arrangement and the fleshy nature of the peach fruit.

## 3. Evolutionary Inference
The phylogeny place *Prunus persica* as a core eudicot, sister to the Rosaceae alliance. Its MADS-box diversification reflects the family's ancestral genome events, contributing to the development of its characteristic floral and fruit traits.

## 4. Methodological Consistency
High support values (1000 replicates) in both NJ and aBayes trees across multiple alignments confirm its correct phylogenetic position and clade assignments.
